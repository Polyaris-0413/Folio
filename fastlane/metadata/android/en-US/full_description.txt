Folio (拾页) is a local reader that pays attention to detail.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, for Android 12 and above.
No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

Features:
- Material Design 3 UI
- Thoughtful animations
- Book title recognition and cleanup
- Table of contents detection
- Text-to-speech reading (system TTS)
- Auto import from your shelf folder
- AI assistance
- Online updates
